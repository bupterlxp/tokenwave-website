Research

The work behind the standard.

Benchmarks are only the visible edge. TokenWave's research spans six directions — from measurement itself to the open models it measures — built in the open with the M-A-P community.

Flagship work
Knowledge benchmark

SuperGPQA

Graduate-level knowledge and reasoning tested across 285 disciplines, far beyond the usual STEM shortlist.
Coding benchmark

NL2Repo-Bench

Whether a coding agent can turn a natural-language brief into a complete working repository.
GUI-agent benchmark

Workflow-GYM

Professional GUI work measured end to end, where one missed stage can invalidate the whole workflow.
Open code LLM

OpenCoder

OpenCoder is an open cookbook for building top-tier code language models: the complete, reproducible recipe — data pipeline, corpus, training details, and checkpoints — behind a code LLM family that matches leading models of its size.
Formal theorem prover

OProver

OProver is a unified framework for agentic formal theorem proving in Lean4: retrieval, compiler feedback, and multi-round repair are built into training itself rather than bolted on at inference.
Looped language models

Ouro

Ouro is a family of looped language models that build reasoning into pretraining: a shared block of layers is applied iteratively in latent space, with learned depth allocation, trained on 7.7T tokens.
Music foundation model

YuE

YuE is M-A-P's open foundation model family for full-song music generation: from lyrics to a complete, structured song — vocals, accompaniment, and multi-minute coherence — released with weights and training recipe.
Web-scale instruction data

MAmmoTH2

MAmmoTH2 shows that the web already contains the instruction data everyone pays to create: it harvests roughly ten million naturally occurring instruction–response pairs from pretraining corpora, then refines them — lifting reasoning benchmarks dramatically without human annotation or frontier-model distillation..

Workflow-GYM: When One Missed Stage Invalidates the Job

Professional desktop work is not a sequence of clicks — it is a chain of dependent stages where an early omission poisons everything downstream. We look at what 338 long-horizon workflows across 56 software environments reveal about computer-use agents, and why even the leader fails two trials out of three.

2026-07-18 · Multimodal Agent · 1 min read

WorldTravel: Would the Day Actually Work?

A travel plan is easy to write and hard to keep. WorldTravel scores multimodal agents on a single unforgiving question — if a traveler followed this itinerary, would it survive contact with opening hours, reservations, and the clock? No model on the board manages it even one time in five.

SuperGPQA: Graduate-Level Knowledge at Frontier Scale

How far does frontier-model knowledge really extend beyond the mainstream disciplines? We examine graduate-level question answering across hundreds of specialized fields and find that long-tail expertise remains the clearest gap between models.

MM-BrowseComp: Multimodal Browsing Beyond Text Trails

Web research is not text-only. MM-BrowseComp asks search agents to follow evidence through images, charts, and pages that no text-only pipeline can shortcut, revealing a wide gap behind text-centric browsing scores.
