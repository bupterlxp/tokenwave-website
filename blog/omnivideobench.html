OmniVideoBench: When Models Must Watch and Listen at Once

2026-05-14 · OmniVideoBench · 1 min read

True video understanding is audio-visual. OmniVideoBench probes whether omni models can integrate what they see with what they hear over long horizons, instead of answering from a single dominant modality.

Play a cooking video with the sound off and you will still follow most of it. Play a lecture, a stand-up set, or a suspense scene with the sound off and you will miss the point entirely. Video understanding was never a vision problem — it is a synchronization problem, and OmniVideoBench is built to test exactly that.

No single modality gets a free ride

The benchmark covers music, speech, and sound effects across videos of widely varying duration, and its questions are constructed so that neither the eye nor the ear alone is sufficient. A model that transcribes audio brilliantly but ignores the frame — or captions frames while deaf to tone and timing — gets caught.

This design choice matters because "omni" models are often graded on the modality they happen to be best at. Averaging over unimodal questions rewards specialization; requiring joint evidence rewards integration. Only the second thing deserves the name omni.
