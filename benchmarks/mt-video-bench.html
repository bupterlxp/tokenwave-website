Abstract

MT-Video-Bench evaluates perception and interaction in multi-turn video dialogues. This page uses Overall from the main table and keeps the six sub-dimensions as auxiliary metrics.

Contributions

  • The paper shows that even strong models remain far from saturated in multi-turn video dialogue.
  • The six sub-metrics help distinguish whether a model struggles more with perception or conversational interaction.

Method & evaluation

  • MT-Video-Bench moves video evaluation from single-turn QA to multi-turn dialogue, focusing on sustained understanding and interaction over videos.
  • It defines six core competencies around perceptivity and interactivity, and curates 1,000 multi-turn video dialogues from diverse domains aligned with realistic applications such as sports analysis and intelligent tutoring.
  • The paper shows large performance gaps between open-source and closed-source MLLMs, while also exposing weaknesses in long-horizon tracking and interactive consistency.
  • MT-Video-Bench therefore serves as an important bridge between standard video QA and more realistic video-agent settings.

Evaluation metrics

Overall — Higher is better. Overall multi-turn video benchmark score. Score range: [0, 100].
