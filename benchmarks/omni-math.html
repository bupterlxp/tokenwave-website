Omni-MATH

Olympiad Math · updated 2026-08-03

Paper ↗ Code & Website ↗ PDF ↗

Abstract

Omni-MATH is a 4,428-problem benchmark of Olympiad-level mathematics organized into 33 sub-domains and 10 difficulty tiers, with Omni-Judge for automated answer checking. It measures competition mathematics specifically, past the saturation of GSM8K and MATH. Results on this page will be populated at the next TokenWave data refresh.

Contributions

  • 4,428 competition-level problems with rigorous human annotation.
  • 33 sub-domains across more than 10 difficulty levels enable fine-grained diagnosis, not one blended score.
  • Ships Omni-Judge, an open evaluator for checking free-form competition answers.

Method & evaluation

  • Problems are curated from Olympiad sources and annotated by domain and difficulty.
  • Models answer free-form; Omni-Judge verifies equivalence against reference answers.
  • Analysis at release showed frontier reasoning models still failing a large share of higher-tier problems.

Evaluation metrics

Accuracy — Higher is better. Judge-verified answer accuracy across the full set. Score range: [0, 100].
