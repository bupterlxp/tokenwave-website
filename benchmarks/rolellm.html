Abstract

RoleLLM benchmarks and enhances role-playing abilities in language models. This page uses the avg score from the main English results table and keeps CUS, RAW, and SPE as finer-grained metrics.

Contributions

  • The work introduces not only a benchmark but also RoleGPT, RoleBench, and enhancement pipelines.
  • The page keeps style, accuracy, and role-specific knowledge as explicit dimensions.

Method & evaluation

  • RoleLLM goes beyond proposing a benchmark and studies how to elicit and improve role-playing ability.
  • The paper organizes the pipeline into role profile construction, role-specific knowledge extraction, RoleGPT prompting, and role-conditioned tuning.
  • Based on this pipeline, it builds RoleBench, a large-scale and fine-grained dataset for role-playing evaluation.
  • It further trains RoleLLaMA and RoleGLM, showing clear gains for open models on role-playing tasks.
  • Its main contribution is integrating evaluation, prompting, data construction, and training into one framework.

Evaluation metrics

avg — Higher is better. Average role-playing score. Score range: [0, 100].
