Abstract

OWL studies large language models for IT operations. This page uses the Average Score reported in the paper.

Contributions

  • OWL is closer to enterprise operations than generic QA.
  • The score is not a percentage, so the page preserves the paper's original scale.

Method & evaluation

  • OWL targets the IT operations domain instead of generic chat or QA scenarios.
  • The paper builds Owl-Instruct and Owl-Bench to train and evaluate a specialized model for IT tasks.
  • It proposes HMCE for extending usable context and adopts a mixture-of-adapter strategy for tuning.
  • Experiments show that OWL outperforms contemporary general-purpose baselines on multiple IT tasks.
  • Its main message is that domain-specific LLMs and benchmarks are necessary for realistic IT operations use cases.

Evaluation metrics

Average Score — Higher is better. Average task score reported by the paper. Score range: [0, 10].
