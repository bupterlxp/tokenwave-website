MTU-Bench

Abstract

MTU-Bench evaluates tool use at multiple granularities. This page uses the most challenging Multi-turn Multi-tool (M-M) setting as the primary leaderboard and also keeps the other three settings.

Contributions

  • M-M is the hardest setting and best reflects complex tool orchestration ability.
  • All four granularities are kept so the page can explain why models lead or lag on the aggregate leaderboard.

Method & evaluation

  • MTU-Bench evaluates tool-use ability while emphasizing differences across multiple interaction granularities.
  • It covers single-turn and multi-turn settings, single-tool and multi-tool settings, as well as out-of-distribution tasks.
  • Unlike many prior benchmarks, it avoids GPT-based or human scoring and uses objective metrics derived from ground truth.
  • The benchmark is built by transforming existing high-quality tasks and is paired with MTU-Instruct for improvement.
  • This makes it both broader in coverage and cheaper to evaluate at scale.

Evaluation metrics

M-M — Higher is better. Multi-turn multi-tool performance. Score range: [0, 100].

The primary leaderboard uses M-M. The paper does not report a single unified overall score, so this page chooses the most representative hard setting as the main metric.
