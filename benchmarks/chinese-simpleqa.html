Abstract

Chinese SimpleQA focuses on Chinese factual question answering. This page uses the F-score reported in the paper.

Contributions

  • The benchmark targets Chinese factuality rather than transferred English knowledge.
  • The page uses F-score to balance answer correctness and completeness.

Method & evaluation

  • Chinese SimpleQA is the first comprehensive benchmark for factual short-form question answering in Chinese, designed to measure how reliably models answer concise fact-seeking questions.
  • It covers 6 broad topics and 99 subtopics, and combines strict quality control, static reference answers, and short QA pairs to keep evaluation reliable and reproducible.
  • The paper uses the benchmark to compare existing LLMs and further studies the effects of model scale, calibration, RAG, and alignment on Chinese factuality.
  • The results show that Chinese factuality rankings do not simply mirror English leaderboards, so Chinese-facing models still require dedicated evaluation and optimization.

Evaluation metrics

F-score — Higher is better. F-score on Chinese SimpleQA. Score range: [0, 100].
