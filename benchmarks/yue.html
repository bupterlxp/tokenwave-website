YuE

Music Generation · updated 2026-08-03

Paper ↗ Code & Website ↗ PDF ↗

Abstract

YuE is M-A-P's open foundation model family for full-song music generation: from lyrics to a complete, structured song — vocals, accompaniment, and multi-minute coherence — released with weights and training recipe. It anchors the open end of the lyrics-to-song problem the way open code models anchored programming.

Contributions

  • Generates complete songs — verse, chorus, vocals, and instrumental accompaniment — from lyrics, at multi-minute length with structural coherence.
  • Supports multiple languages and vocal styles, with in-context conditioning for style, melody, and voice.
  • Fully open release: model weights, inference stack, and recipe, making it the reference open lyrics-to-song system.

Method & evaluation

  • A dual-track token design models vocals and accompaniment jointly while keeping them separable.
  • Long-context audio tokenization sustains song-level structure instead of short clips.
  • Evaluation combines human preference studies with audio-quality and lyric-alignment measures against commercial systems.

Evaluation metrics

Song-level quality — Higher is better. Tracked qualitatively via the paper's human preference and alignment studies.
