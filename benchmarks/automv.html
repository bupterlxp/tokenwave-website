Abstract

AutoMV turns a full song into a coherent music video with a multi-agent system — and ships the first music-to-video evaluation benchmark: four categories and twelve expert criteria, with an LLM judge that tracks professional raters. TokenWave anchors the Audio & Video Editor career to it.

Contributions

  • The first open benchmark for music-to-video quality: Music Content, Technical, Post-production, and Art, refined into twelve criteria scored 1–5.
  • LLM-Score, an automatic judge whose ratings largely align with independent musicians and music-industry professionals.
  • A generation system to match: screenwriter and director agents, a character bank for identity consistency, and a verifier that sends failed shots back for regeneration.

Method & evaluation

  • Evaluation runs on 30 professionally released songs across English, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.
  • Expert raters watch full videos with audio and score every sub-criterion; LLM-Score approximates the same rubric automatically.
  • Cross-system comparison also reports audio-visual alignment via ImageBind score.

Evaluation metrics

ImageBind score — Higher is better. Audio-visual alignment between the generated video and the song; the paper's cross-system comparison metric. Score range: [0, 100].
