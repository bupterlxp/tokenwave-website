Abstract

ConceptMath is a bilingual concept-wise benchmark for mathematical reasoning. This page uses the Avg column from the paper's main table.

Contributions

  • The core value of ConceptMath is concept-level diagnosis rather than one coarse math score.
  • The page uses Avg to avoid fragmenting the site into too many education-system and prompting variants.

Method & evaluation

  • ConceptMath moves math evaluation beyond average accuracy by measuring performance at the level of individual mathematical concepts.
  • The benchmark is bilingual in Chinese and English and organizes problems under a concept hierarchy, so models can be analyzed both globally and at fine granularity.
  • The paper shows that many models with strong average scores on traditional math benchmarks still exhibit severe weaknesses, or even catastrophic failures, on specific basic concepts.
  • As a result, ConceptMath serves not only as a leaderboard but also as a diagnostic tool for targeted improvement and concept-level fine-tuning.

Evaluation metrics

Avg — Higher is better. Average ConceptMath score across systems and prompt settings. Score range: [0, 100].
