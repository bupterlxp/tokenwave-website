Abstract

OmniBench targets the future of universal omni-language models. This page uses the Overall score under the image-and-audio setting.

Contributions

  • The benchmark emphasizes unified omni models rather than ordinary vision-language systems.
  • The page uses the image-and-audio setting as a single consistent primary leaderboard.

Method & evaluation

  • OmniBench evaluates whether models can jointly recognize, interpret, and reason over text, image, and audio within the same context.
  • The paper refers to systems with this capability as omni-language models and uses carefully human-annotated examples so that correct answers truly require tri-modal integration.
  • The results show that many baselines, including open-source systems, still perform poorly in genuine tri-modal settings, even when alternative textual representations of image or audio are provided.
  • OmniBench therefore highlights a core weakness of current multimodal training pipelines: they still struggle to build a coherent shared context across three modalities at once.

Evaluation metrics

Image & Audio Overall — Higher is better. Overall score under the image-and-audio evaluation setting. Score range: [0, 100].
