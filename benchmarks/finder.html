Abstract

This work studies how close current systems are to genuinely useful deep research agents. The displayed leaderboard uses the Checklist Pass Rate reported in the paper.

Contributions

  • The paper cares about whether the research process is genuinely completed, not only whether the final answer looks plausible.
  • The page uses checklist pass rate because it matches the process-oriented evaluation design.

Method & evaluation

  • FINDER focuses on whether deep research agents can produce analyst-level reports rather than simple answers.
  • It introduces 100 human-curated research tasks together with 419 structured checklist items.
  • These checklist items standardize report structure, analytical depth, and factual grounding.
  • Based on around 1,000 generated reports, the paper also proposes the DEFT failure taxonomy.
  • The main finding is that current systems struggle more with evidence integration, verification, and planning than with task understanding.

Evaluation metrics

Checklist Pass Rate — Higher is better. Checklist-based pass rate reported by the paper. Score range: [0, 100].
