KOR-Bench

🏆 o1-preview: 72.88 · Reasoning · updated 2026-04-15

Paper ↗ PDF ↗

Abstract

KOR-Bench evaluates knowledge-orthogonal reasoning by reducing the effect of prior domain knowledge. This page uses the Overall score from the main results table.

Contributions

  • The benchmark aims to separate actual reasoning ability from memorized knowledge.
  • The page uses the main Overall score and does not mix in the additional integrated-task setting.

Method & evaluation

  • KOR-Bench introduces the idea of knowledge-orthogonal reasoning, aiming to evaluate rule understanding and reasoning ability while minimizing dependence on domain-specific knowledge.
  • It includes five task families: operation, logic, cipher, puzzle, and counterfactual, all designed around novel rule-driven problems.
  • The paper shows that this benchmark can better reveal whether a model is genuinely reasoning instead of leaning on memorized knowledge patterns.
  • It also studies stepwise prompting, self-correction, and task-specific tricks, making KOR-Bench a useful foundation for general reasoning evaluation.

Evaluation metrics

Overall — Higher is better. Overall reasoning accuracy on KOR-Bench. Score range: [0, 100].
