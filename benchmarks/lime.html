LIME

🏆 InternVL2-40B: 66.85 · Image Understanding · updated 2026-04-15

Paper ↗ Code & Website ↗ PDF ↗

Abstract

LIME curates a more discriminative and efficient multimodal benchmark by filtering low-value and leakage-prone samples. This page uses the LIME Overall score reported in the paper.

Contributions

  • LIME is valuable not merely because it rescales scores, but because it recurates the benchmark samples.
  • The paper reports a 0.91 correlation between LIME overall score and WildVision Elo.

Method & evaluation

  • LIME is motivated by the idea that MLLM evaluation should be smaller but more informative, because many existing benchmarks contain samples that are too easy or weakly discriminative.
  • It uses a semi-automatic pipeline to remove uninformative items, reduce answer leakage, and keep tasks that genuinely require image understanding.
  • The paper shows that this refined benchmark cuts both sample count and evaluation time substantially while improving the ability to distinguish models.
  • It also argues that traditional automatic metrics such as CIDEr are insufficient for MLLM caption evaluation, which makes LIME relevant not only as a benchmark but also as a methodology for benchmark design.

Evaluation metrics

LIME Overall — Higher is better. Overall score on the curated LIME benchmark. Score range: [0, 100].
