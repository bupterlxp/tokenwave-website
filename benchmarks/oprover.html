OProver

Leader — OProver-32B · 93.3 · Formal Math · updated 2026-08-03

Paper ↗ Code & Website ↗ PDF ↗

Abstract

OProver is a unified framework for agentic formal theorem proving in Lean4: retrieval, compiler feedback, and multi-round repair are built into training itself rather than bolted on at inference. OProver-32B sets the open-weight state of the art on MiniF2F and outperforms a 671B prover across all five of its evaluations.

Contributions

  • Unifies retrieval augmentation, Lean4 compiler feedback, and iterative repair with the training strategy and data construction, instead of treating them as inference-time modules.
  • Releases OProofs: 1.77M Lean statements with 6.86M compiler-verified proofs and serialized proving trajectories, including failed attempts and repairs.
  • OProver-32B reaches 93.3 on MiniF2F (Pass@32), state of the art among open-weight whole-proof provers, beating DeepSeek-Prover-V2 at 671B on all five reported benchmarks.

Method & evaluation

  • Continued pretraining is followed by iterative post-training: each round runs agentic proving, indexes newly verified proofs into the retrieval memory, converts repair trajectories to SFT data, and sends unresolved cases to RL.
  • At inference the prover retrieves compiler-verified proofs, attempts a proof, and revises against Lean4 compiler feedback across rounds.
  • Pass@32 rises monotonically through the loop: OProver-8B climbs from 79.5 to 91.8 on MiniF2F across three rounds.

Evaluation metrics

MiniF2F Pass@32 — Higher is better. Share of MiniF2F-Test statements proven within 32 samples; proofs must check in Lean4. Score range: [0, 100].
