Code & Website ↗

Abstract

DR3-Eval is a realistic and reproducible benchmark for deep research evaluation. This page uses the 512k Overall score from the official project page.

Contributions

  • Even under the strongest current systems, the 512k setting remains challenging.
  • The work emphasizes realism, reproducibility, and failure analysis rather than a single score.

Method & evaluation

  • DR3-Eval is designed to make deep research evaluation more realistic, controllable, and reproducible at the same time.
  • It starts from real user multimodal files, builds a static sandbox for each task, and generates queries through reverse construction.
  • The benchmark centers on multi-file report generation rather than short QA or isolated retrieval.
  • Its evaluation protocol covers information recall, factual accuracy, citation coverage, instruction following, and depth quality.
  • The main contribution is addressing the long-standing tension between realism and reproducibility in deep research evaluation.

Evaluation metrics

Overall (512k) — Higher is better. Overall score under the 512k evaluation setting. Score range: [0, 100].
