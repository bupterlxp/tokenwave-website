Abstract

ViDiC evaluates video difference captioning by measuring how well models describe fine-grained differences and similarities between videos. This page uses the Average score from the main table.

Method & evaluation

  • This work extends difference captioning from images to videos, requiring models to describe differences, similarities, and temporal changes between paired videos.
  • It emphasizes fine-grained factors such as position, background, camera motion, and filming technique, making the task more comparative than standard captioning.
  • The main results show strong performance from leading language models, but clear weaknesses remain on fine-grained categories.

Evaluation metrics

Average — Higher is better. Average score on the main ViDiC results table. Score range: [0, 100].
