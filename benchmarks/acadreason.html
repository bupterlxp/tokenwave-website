Abstract

ACADREASON evaluates model reasoning on real academic research problems across disciplines. This page uses the average score from the paper's main table.

Method & evaluation

  • This work uses real academic research problems as a challenging testbed across computer science, economics, law, mathematics, and philosophy.
  • Unlike exam-style benchmarks, ACADREASON emphasizes open-ended research tasks, multi-source synthesis, and long-horizon reasoning.
  • The results show that even leading models still achieve limited overall performance on genuine academic reasoning problems.

Evaluation metrics

Average Score — Higher is better. Average score value from the main ACADREASON results table. Score range: [0, 100].
