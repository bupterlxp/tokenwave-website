MAmmoTH2

Synthetic Data · updated 2026-08-03

Paper ↗ Code & Website ↗ PDF ↗

Abstract

MAmmoTH2 shows that the web already contains the instruction data everyone pays to create: it harvests roughly ten million naturally occurring instruction–response pairs from pretraining corpora, then refines them — lifting reasoning benchmarks dramatically without human annotation or frontier-model distillation.

Contributions

  • A three-stage pipeline — recall candidate documents, extract Q&A pairs, refine them — yields the ~10M-example WebInstruct corpus from the open web.
  • Requires neither crowdworkers nor distillation from proprietary models, changing the cost structure of instruction data.
  • Tuning on WebInstruct lifts base models sharply on reasoning suites, and MAmmoTH2-Plus variants remain competitive instruction models at their scale.

Method & evaluation

  • Recall finds instruction-shaped documents in web corpora; extraction isolates the Q&A; refinement cleans and completes the pairs.
  • The paper reports large math-reasoning gains for tuned models against their own baselines.
  • Follows MAmmoTH, whose MathInstruct corpus hybridized chain-of-thought and program-of-thought rationales.

Evaluation metrics

Instruction-data yield — Higher is better. Tracked qualitatively: corpus scale and downstream reasoning gains reported by the paper.
