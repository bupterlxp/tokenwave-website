FormalMATH

Leader — Kimina-Prover · 16.46 · Formal Math · updated 2026-08-03

Paper ↗ Code & Website ↗ PDF ↗

Abstract

FormalMATH is a large-scale Lean4 benchmark of 5,560 formally verified statements, from Olympiad challenges to undergraduate theorems across algebra, calculus, number theory, and discrete mathematics. The strongest prover reaches 16.46% under practical sampling budgets — formal reasoning remains largely open.

Contributions

  • 5,560 formally verified Lean4 problems — over twenty times the scale of MiniF2F's test split.
  • A human-in-the-loop autoformalization pipeline: specialist LLMs draft statements, multi-LLM semantic checks and negation-based disproof filter them, and IMO-medalist-level experts verify.
  • Reveals pronounced domain bias: provers do far better in algebra than calculus, and over-rely on simplified automation tactics.

Method & evaluation

  • Statements are autoformalized from natural-language problems, then filtered by semantic verification and disproof of negations.
  • Provers are evaluated under fixed sampling budgets; a proof must check in Lean4.
  • The paper also finds natural-language solution guidance can hurt formal proof success.

Evaluation metrics

Success rate — Higher is better. Share of statements formally proven within the sampling budget (pass@32 for whole-proof generation). Score range: [0, 100].
