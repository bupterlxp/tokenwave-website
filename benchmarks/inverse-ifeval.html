Abstract

Inverse IFEval studies whether models can break stubborn training conventions and still follow real instructions. This page uses the Overall Score from the English main table.

Method & evaluation

  • This work goes beyond conventional formatting compliance and explicitly designs inverse instructions that conflict with common training conventions.
  • The tasks test whether models can still obey real user intent under correction, counter-conventional formatting, dialogue intervention, and minimal-modification requirements.
  • The results show that even the strongest models still have clear weaknesses on inverse instruction following.
