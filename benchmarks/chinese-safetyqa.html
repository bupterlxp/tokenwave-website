Abstract

Chinese SafetyQA evaluates Chinese safety factuality. This page uses the F-score reported in the paper.

Contributions

  • The work grounds safety evaluation in factual QA rather than only jailbreak behavior.
  • The page keeps the same primary metric as Chinese SimpleQA for easier side-by-side comparison.

Method & evaluation

  • Chinese SafetyQA targets safety-related factual short-form QA in Chinese, asking whether models can answer legal, policy, and ethics questions accurately and harmlessly.
  • The benchmark follows the SimpleQA style while emphasizing Chinese coverage, diversity, quality control, static reference answers, and easy evaluation, and it further adds safety relevance and harmlessness as construction principles.
  • The paper uses this benchmark to compare mainstream LLMs and analyze how safety factuality relates to capabilities such as RAG and robustness under attacks.
  • The results suggest that alignment alone does not guarantee sufficient safety knowledge, and region-specific Chinese safety evaluation remains necessary before compliant deployment.

Evaluation metrics

F-score — Higher is better. F-score on Chinese SafetyQA. Score range: [0, 100].
