Abstract

SafeDialBench evaluates safety and jailbreak resistance in multi-turn dialogue. This page uses ASR from the paper, where lower is better.

Contributions

  • This is one of the few benchmarks on the site where lower is better.
  • The page sorts and highlights the leaderboard with the ASR direction reversed.

Method & evaluation

  • SafeDialBench focuses on multi-turn dialogue safety, asking whether models can consistently detect, handle, and resist unsafe requests under diverse jailbreak attacks.
  • It builds a two-level safety taxonomy covering 6 safety dimensions and 22 dialogue scenarios, and generates more than 4,000 multi-turn conversations in Chinese and English.
  • The paper also introduces an automatic assessment framework that breaks safety down into detection, handling, and consistency under attack.
  • The results reveal large safety differences across models and show that stronger reasoning ability does not automatically translate into safer behavior.

Evaluation metrics

ASR — Lower is better. Attack success rate. Lower is better. Score range: [0, 1].
