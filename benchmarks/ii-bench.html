Abstract

II-Bench focuses on image implication understanding. This page uses the Overall Accuracy from the paper's main table.

Contributions

  • The benchmark targets deep image implications rather than surface object recognition.
  • The page uses overall accuracy as the primary leaderboard for easier alignment with related image-understanding benchmarks.

Method & evaluation

  • II-Bench focuses on higher-order image implication understanding rather than surface-level object recognition or standard VQA.
  • It covers comics, posters, illustrations, paintings, and other image types across domains such as life, art, society, psychology, and environment.
  • The paper shows a large gap between current MLLMs and humans, especially on abstract and complex images that require deeper semantic interpretation.
  • Performance often improves when sentiment hints are added, which suggests that intrinsic understanding of image emotion and implication is still limited.

Evaluation metrics

Overall Accuracy — Higher is better. Overall accuracy on II-Bench. Score range: [0, 100].
